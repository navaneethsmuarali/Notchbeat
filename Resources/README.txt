NOTCHBEAT
A Dynamic Island for YouTube Music on your Mac.

A black pill at the top centre of your screen shows the song playing in
YouTube Music. Hover over it to get:
  - artwork, song title and artist
  - a progress bar you can drag to skip
  - previous / play-pause / next buttons
  - round app bubbles to switch to your open apps and windows
  - tabs for Files, Clipboard and Calendar (see below)

REQUIREMENTS
  - A Mac running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer (Apple Silicon or Intel)
  - Google Chrome, with YouTube Music open in a tab or as the installed
    YouTube Music Chrome app (music.youtube.com)

INSTALL
1. Drag Notchbeat.app into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click Notchbeat. macOS will say it "cannot be verified"
   (the app is not from the App Store). Click Done.
3. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
   "Open Anyway" next to Notchbeat. Confirm with your password.
   You only need to do this once.

ONE-TIME SETUP
1. Chrome setting
   Click a normal Chrome window (not the YouTube Music app window), then in
   the menu bar at the very top of the screen choose:
     View -> Developer -> Allow JavaScript from Apple Events
   so it shows a tick.
2. When macOS asks "Notchbeat wants to control Google Chrome", click Allow.
3. Optional, for jumping to individual windows:
   System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Accessibility -> turn on Notchbeat.

USING IT
  - Play a song in YouTube Music. The island appears at the top centre.
  - Hover to expand. Move away to close.
  - Click a bubble to switch to that app. A number on a bubble means the
    app has several windows; click it to pick one.
  - Right-click a bubble to pin or unpin it.
  - The music-note icon in the menu bar has controls, App Shortcuts
    (add your favourite apps), Launch at Login, and Quit.

FILE TRAY + AIRDROP  (Files tab)
  - Drag any file toward the top centre of the screen; the island opens.
    Drop it to keep it in the tray. Drag it back out to use it anywhere.
  - Drop files on the blue AirDrop tile to send them with AirDrop.
  - Right-click a file for Open / Show in Finder / AirDrop / Remove.

CLIPBOARD HISTORY  (Clipboard tab)
  - Remembers your last 60 copies (text, links, files, images).
    Click one to copy it again. Right-click to delete it.
  - Stays on your Mac. Passwords copied from password managers are skipped.
    Images are only kept until you quit the app.
  - Turn it off any time: menu bar music-note icon -> Clipboard History.

CALENDAR  (Calendar tab)
  - Click "Connect Calendar" once and allow access.
  - Shows today's and tomorrow's events. Zoom / Google Meet / Teams /
    Webex meetings get a green Join button.
  - 10 minutes before a meeting, the small island shows a countdown.
  - Works with any calendar added to the Mac Calendar app (iCloud, Google,
    Outlook, ...).

MORE IN THE ISLAND
  - Up Next: in the Music tab click the list button to see and play
    upcoming songs.
  - Glances: small info on both sides of the island (time, battery, ...).
    Choose them in Settings.
  - System tab: CPU, memory, network and battery graphs, plus Bluetooth
    device batteries (AirPods, keyboards, mice).
  - Notes tab: a scratchpad that saves as you type. "Save to Apple Notes"
    copies it into the Notes app.
  - Agents tab: shows when Claude Code or Codex is working, needs your
    approval, or has finished (with a sound). Reads only local logs.
  - Pop-up alerts: AirPods/devices connecting, low battery, charging.

FLOATING PLAYER
  - Turn on with the picture-in-picture button in the island or the menu
    bar menu. Drag it anywhere. Three sizes (... menu). Swipe left/right
    with two fingers on it to skip songs.

CLIPBOARD SHORTCUT
  - Press Shift-Command-V anywhere to open clipboard history.
    Up/Down + Return pastes; 1-9 picks directly; Esc closes.
    Pasting automatically needs Accessibility permission.

SCREEN RECORDING
  - Click the record button in the island. A red timer shows while
    recording; click it to stop. The video is saved to
    Movies/Notchbeat Recordings and added to the Files tab.
  - First time: allow Notchbeat under System Settings -> Privacy &
    Security -> Screen & System Audio Recording. Needs macOS 15+.

SETTINGS
  - Gear button in the island, or menu bar icon -> Settings.
    Glass or black island, highlight colour, glances, which tabs show,
    shortcut, alerts, launch at login.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  - Island says "Setup needed": click it and follow the steps.
  - Nothing appears: make sure a song is actually playing in Chrome, and
    that "Allow JavaScript from Apple Events" is ticked.
  - Notchbeat does not read your Google account or passwords. For music it only reads
    the song info shown on the YouTube Music page and presses its buttons.
